Gaps & scenes

Uncovered time is not blank — the renderer holds the preceding scene across it, frozen in staging until the next scene starts. A gap you would rather keep can instead be covered by the next scene's transition, in Scene Casting. {% if audio_available %}Clicking a gap fills the Add scene form and parks the playhead at its start, so ▶ Play auditions what is actually there.{% endif %}
