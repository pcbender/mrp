4Castle has been circling political anxiety for a while, but here they come right at it: three verses mapping a single figure's occupation of every room in the house, each chorus tightening the screws until the final one swaps 'some day' for 'that day' and the whole song pivots on a single word. The arrangement earns that pivot, building from a spare clean guitar figure and warm bass through widening harmony stacks, and the bridge, 'But every house has windows / And every lock can turn,' lands with the quiet confidence of a line that knows it doesn't need to shout. The spoken interlude, a mumbled 'I think I'm really, if you think about it... the best ever,' is the kind of move that could tip into satire but instead reads as documentary, all the more unsettling for how casually it drops in. A highlight.
