Here's the rare song about the terror of the blank page that doesn't flinch from its own subject. Over palm-muted eighth notes at a driving 143 in F minor, PCBender lays out the writer's oldest fear as a confession: "But it plays back like a record / That I've already heard." The verses stay close and sparse, then the chorus opens into full power chords and a strained tenor belt on "Can I borrow your brilliance / And claim it as mine," and that lift is the whole engine of the track. What keeps it honest is the outro, where the drums pull to half-time and the question resolves without cheating: "Maybe I'm not a thief, just a person asking why." The self-flagellation runs a touch long across two nearly identical choruses before the bridge earns its turn, but that final couplet, "Let me get it right / In a way that's new," lands the argument the whole song has been building. A highlight, and a smart one.
