The structure here is the whole argument: four movements, each opening in its own key, all pulled home to F# minor for a coda that lands on "you are when / beginnings / begin." The intro does something quietly ingenious, laying out each movement's melody in advance so that when they return with variations you feel the recognition before you register it, the way a good sermon plants its ending in its opening line. The odd meter gives the whole thing a slow rocking gait, and the single repeated word "Sentia" works as a hinge, that intervallic rising vocal line lifting a little higher each time it comes around. Movement 2, with its "bow leans into the string" tension and the synth solo that follows, is the most focused stretch; movement 3 leans harder on Latin abstraction ("solutus, unfastened, unbound, wet clay") and asks more patience of you before the distorted guitar arrives to break the spell. The last minute is the payoff worth waiting for: the heavy instrumentation falls away entirely, leaving an acoustic figure and layered harmonies to close the circle. Not a track you play in the background, and not every movement carries equal weight, but the craft in how it's assembled rewards the room and the volume. A grower that opens up on the third listen.
